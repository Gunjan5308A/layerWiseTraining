Layer-Wise Training Architecture

nanoGPT — 1 active layer (LR ramp 50%→100%) + 1 prev layer (10% LR) + 10 frozen layers per step

Active (LR ramp 50%→100%)

Frozen (0 LR, requires_grad=False)
Always trainable (wte, wpe, ln_f)
EMA sync (end of cycle)

Layer-Wise Phase

Each of 12 layers trains for 10 steps at a time.
• Active layer: requires_grad=True, LR ramps 50%→100% over 5 steps
• Previous layer: requires_grad=True, 10% LR (soft freeze)
• All others: requires_grad=False, LR=0 (hard frozen)
• wte, wpe, ln_f: always full LR
• 120 steps per full cycle (12 layers × 10 steps)

EMA Sync (end of cycle)

After all 12 layers train once, EMA sync runs.
• Each inactive layer blended toward nearest trained layer
• w = 0.99 × w + 0.01 × trained_w
• No full unfreeze — VRAM stays flat
• Zero extra gradient tensors
• Then cycle resets to layer 0
Speed: 500ms

Architecture Summary

ALGORITHM
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  for each cycle:
    for layer i in [0..11]:
      L{i}   = ACTIVE  (requires_grad=True,  LR = 6e-4 * ramp)
      L{i-1} = SOFT    (requires_grad=True,  LR = 6e-5  10%)
      all others = FROZEN (requires_grad=False, LR = 0)
      run 10 optimizer steps (LR ramp 50%→100% over first 5)

    EMA sync: each layer w = 0.99*w + 0.01*nearest_trained_w
    reset to layer 0, repeat

VRAM BREAKDOWN (per training step)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Gradients:  only 2 layers (active + prev) = ~4.5M params
  Optimizer:  only 2 layers (AdamW state = 2x mem)
  Activations: 1 micro-batch at a time (batch_size=1)
  Total:       ~1.2 GB peak on 4GB VRAM GPU

COMPARED TO OLD LOGIC
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Old: soft-freeze ALL 11 inactive layers at 1% LR
       → gradients computed for all layers (wasteful)
       → global sync phase: unfreeze ALL, full LR
       → VRAM spikes during sync

  New: hard-freeze 10 layers (LR=0, no grads)
       prev layer at 10% LR (keeps momentum)
       active layer LR ramp 50%→100% (prevents loss spike)
       EMA sync instead of full unfreeze
       → VRAM stays flat entire training

EARLY STOPPING
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stop when val loss <= 1e-4
